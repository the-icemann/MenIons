{{ company.name }} delivers parcels for shops in Kampala, booked on WhatsApp. This notice explains what personal data we hold, why, and for how long, as the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 requires.

Who else sees it

  • The rider doing a delivery sees the shop's pickup details and the customer's name, number and location.
  • The shop sees the progress of its own deliveries.
  • Service providers that carry the data for us: Meta (WhatsApp) and Kapso for messages, pawaPay and the mobile networks for payments, and our hosting provider. Some of them store data outside Uganda.
  • The Uganda Revenue Authority receives the details on each tax receipt (EFRIS).

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Customers' locations and directions, parcel photos and the raw WhatsApp messages are deleted {{ retention_days }} days after a delivery is finished.
  • Receipts are financial records. They cannot be changed or deleted and are kept for as long as tax law requires.
  • Payment records are kept for the same reason.
